Risk disclosure

You can lose money

Leveraged investing in forex, indices, metals, crypto and shares can result in losses larger than expected, and in some products losses can exceed the amount deposited.

  • Leverage magnifies gains and losses. A small market move can have a large effect on equity.
  • Drawdowns happen. Equity can fall for days or weeks. Public figures include losing days.
  • Correlation rises in stress. Assets that normally move apart can fall together in a sell-off.
  • Gaps and slippage. Stops are not guaranteed. Prices can jump past them, especially around news and market opens.
  • Technology risk. Platform, broker, data or internet failures can delay or prevent orders and alerts.
  • Crypto risk. Digital assets are volatile and lightly regulated in many places.
  • Past results are not a reliable guide to future results. Targets are not promises.
