What we do

An investment process built like software.

Every decision comes from a written rule. Every position has a stop. Every result is on a public page.

Automated strategies

Our strategies read price structure, place orders and manage positions through MetaTrader 5, with no discretionary overrides in the moment.

Fixed risk per position

Position size is set from the stop distance and a fixed risk budget, converted into the account currency, so one position can only cost what we planned.

Live, public reporting

Equity move, daily P&L, open positions and charts are published on a dashboard anyone can open, not in a monthly PDF.

Diversification

Different markets, different drivers.

A portfolio of assets that all rise and fall together is one bet, however many symbols it has. We are rebuilding the book so the positions are driven by different things.

Equity indices

Regional indices

US, European, UK and Australian indices, so one region's bad week does not decide the month.

Metals

Gold and silver

Safe-haven demand and inflation expectations move metals on a different clock from equities.

Digital assets

Bitcoin

Liquidity and crypto-specific flows give a return stream with its own rhythm.

Single stocks

Selected shares

Individual companies chosen by rule, rotated out once they have paid, not held on hope.
